CHATRAPATI SHIVAJI MAHARAJ

"प्रतिपच्चंद्रलेखेव वर्धिष्णुर्विश्ववंदिता ।

शाहसुनोः शिवस्यैषा मुद्रा भद्राय राजते ।।"

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (February 19, 1627 – April 3, 1680) was not just any ruler; he was a Divine incarnation sent to safeguard Hindu Dharma. He founded Hindavi Swarajya — a sovereign Hindu state — with Kshatradharma, guidance from Saints like Samarth Ramdas Swami, and the ideals taught by his mother Jijabai. Against Mughal oppression, he constructed impregnable forts, built a navy to shield our shores, employed ganimi kava (guerrilla warfare) to defeat numerically superior forces, decisively ended the terror of Afzal Khan in a display of wits and valour, and established an ideal Kingdom by protecting temples, scriptures, Brahmins, women, and Gau Mata. He outlawed cow slaughter in his kingdom and adopted the title Go-Brahmana-Pratipalaka, that is, the protector of cows and Brahmins.

    •

Major Achievements & Milestones

Rise to Power:
Captured his first fort (Torna Fort) at a young age and built an independent kingdom by challenging the Adil Shahi Sultanate of Bijapur and the Mughal Empire under Aurangzeb.
Military Innovations:
Pioneered guerrilla warfare (Ganimi Kava) and utilized local terrain and rapid cavalry movements.
Father of the Indian Navy:
Built a strong naval force and coastal forts to protect the Konkan coastline.
